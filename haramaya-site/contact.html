The Main Campus Health Center is located in front of the Muslim Student Cafeteria. Visitors can use the cafeteria and the main parking lot as nearby landmarks.

  • Find the Muslim Student Cafeteria.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Haramaya University Health Center located?

In front of the Muslim Student Cafeteria, Haramaya University Main Campus, Ethiopia.

What is the nearest landmark?

How can I contact the Health Center?

Call the office on +251 255 530 362 or the mobile on +251 904 277 500, or use the contact form.
