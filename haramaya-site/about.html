Who we are

Haramaya University Health Center is the university’s healthcare facility. We are committed to providing accessible, affordable, quality healthcare, with a patient-centered approach and qualified healthcare professionals.

  • Haramaya University students
  • University staff
  • The surrounding community

Our story

From a university clinic to an independent directorate.

Beginning

Haramaya University first provided healthcare to regular and summer students through a clinic.

2005

Establishment

The clinic was accredited by the Oromia Health Bureau as a health center.

Early development

The health center began with two part-time physicians, seven clinical nurses, three laboratory technicians, and other clinical and support staff.

2019

Institutional development

After restructuring in line with university and government directions, the health center became an independent directorate.

Growth and development

The center continues to work on healthcare quality, accessibility, affordability, equity and good governance.

Mission and vision

Our mission

“To provide student and community patient-centered healthcare with compassionate, comprehensive, affordable, excellent in quality, service, and accessible health care to all students and the community.”

Our vision

“We will strive to be the best health center in our country university health center and beyond with affordable accessible primary health care in a manner that exceeds patient expectations.”

Our core values

Principles reflected in the health center’s published institutional information.

Accessibility

Making healthcare services accessible to students and the community.

Affordability

Supporting access to affordable healthcare.

Quality care

Providing effective and quality healthcare services.

Compassion

Treating patients with care, kindness and respect.

Equity

Supporting fair access to healthcare services.

Patient-centered care

Considering patients’ needs and well-being in healthcare decisions.

Professionalism

Supporting responsible and qualified healthcare delivery.

Good governance

Promoting accountable and organized management.
