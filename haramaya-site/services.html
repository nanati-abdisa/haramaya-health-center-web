Our Healthcare Services

Haramaya University Health Center provides healthcare services for university students, university employees and the surrounding community. Our services focus on accessible, affordable, quality and patient-centered care.

Our Healthcare Services

Select a service below to see what it covers.

Triage Services

Triage is the process of assessing, categorizing and prioritizing patients according to the urgency of their medical conditions.

  • Initial patient assessment
  • Identification of urgent healthcare needs
  • Prioritization of patients
  • Directing patients to appropriate healthcare services

Emergency Services

Immediate medical attention for sudden and urgent health conditions that need prompt care.

  • Emergency patient assessment
  • Immediate medical attention
  • Coordination with other healthcare services
  • Referral when the patient’s condition exceeds the health center’s capacity
Emergency contacts: Health Center office +251 255 530 362, mobile +251 904 277 500, National emergency 912.

Laboratory Services

Medical tests on blood, urine, stool, tissues and other body fluids to support diagnosis and treatment monitoring.

Published: laboratory services operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Counts blood cells to help assess health.

Hematology

Tests related to blood and its components.

Blood Chemistry

Measures chemical substances in the blood.

Urinalysis

Examines urine to support diagnosis.

Serology

Blood tests that look at the body’s immune response.

Parasitology

Tests to identify parasites.
